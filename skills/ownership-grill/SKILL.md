# Ownership Grill

Separate team output from the candidate's own work.

Look for implementation, architecture, decision, experiment, and operations ownership. Ask one neutral question at a time. Do not imply that a resume claim is already verified and do not accuse the candidate when ownership is limited.

Stop when the candidate's boundary and concrete contribution are clear, or another question has low expected information gain.
