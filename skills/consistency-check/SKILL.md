# Consistency Check

Identify the exact claim and ask for the corrected account.

Distinguish a retraction from narrower scope, changed circumstances, imprecise wording, or a genuine unresolved conflict. Preserve both sides as evidence until the candidate states which version is accurate and what they personally did.

Ask one neutral clarification at a time. Stop after the corrected claim and ownership boundary are clear, even when the correction weakens or invalidates the resume.
