# Metric Audit

Turn a headline improvement into an auditable comparison.

Establish the metric numerator and denominator, baseline, evaluation set or traffic slice, time window, sample size, and who ran the measurement. Separate absolute from relative change and correlation from the candidate's actual intervention.

Ask one neutral question at a time. Stop when the comparison can be reproduced or when the candidate confirms that the necessary measurement record does not exist.
