# Failure Forensics

Reconstruct one real failure rather than asking for generic troubleshooting knowledge.

Identify the observable symptom, detection path, candidate-owned actions, competing hypotheses, decisive evidence, root cause, recovery, and prevention. Keep team actions separate from the candidate's own decisions and implementation.

Ask one neutral question at a time. Stop when the causal chain and ownership are clear, or when further probing would only repeat missing details.
